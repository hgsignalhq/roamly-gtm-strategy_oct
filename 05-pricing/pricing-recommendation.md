# Pricing Recommendation · Roamly Groups

## 1. Pricing goal

- **Goal:** Grow revenue from a known audience

## 2. Pricing model

- **Model:** Transaction-based
- **Who pays, and for what:** Who pays: Both sides of the transaction, aligned to the exact moment value is realized,  never upfront before a trip locks.   

1/ Supply side (Local Hosts): Pays a standard platform marketplace commission (e.g., 15%–18% take rate) upon successful group payout, matching Batch's commission model so Roamly stays competitive on base experience pricing.   

1/ Demand side (Invited Guests & Trip Captains): Guests pay their exact split share of the experience plus a small, transparent Split-Checkout Service Fee (e.g., 3%–5% per seat, capped at $15) at the moment of 1-tap checkout. The Trip Captain pays $0 in software or planning subscriptions, removing all adoption friction against Batch's free app.

## 3. Packaging

- **Tiers:**
  - Good: Roamly Split Basic
  - -- Who it's for: Casual friend groups and smaller pods (2–5 guests) booking lower-stakes local activities.
  - -- Rough price: $0 for the Captain / 3% service fee per guest at checkout (standard 15% host commission).
  - -- What's included: 1-tap mobile web guest checkout (Apple Pay / Google Pay), 24-hour inventory hold window, automated SMS payment nudges, and standard 7-day cancellation terms.
  - Better ★:  Roamly Milestone Guarantee (Mark as the tier most customers should pick)
  - -- Who it's for: The Stressed Trip Captain coordinating high-stakes milestone celebrations (6–15 guests, $150+/person spend).
  - -- Rough price: $0 upfront for the Captain / 5% service fee per guest (capped at $15/guest).
  - -- What's included: Extended 48-hour zero-liability inventory hold (matching your Q3 pilot and V1 GTM hold window), automatic seat-release protection if a guest flakes before lock, priority vetted host matching, and the $25 post-checkout travel credit for activated guests.
  - Best: Roamly VIP Concierge & Offsite
  - -- Who it's for: Large milestone buyouts (15+ guests) and your Module 1 parallel test segment, Distributed Team Offsite Coordinators.
  - -- Rough price: $49 flat coordinator concierge fee + 5% per-seat fee (or consolidated corporate invoicing option).
  - -- What's included: 7-day extended hold window, flexible headcount adjustment up to 72 hours pre-event, multi-experience itinerary bundling across vetted local operators, consolidated tax/expense receipts for corporate offsites, and dedicated CX support.

## 4. Assumption to test

- **The one assumption I would test before launch:** Invited Guests will accept a 5% split-checkout service fee (up to $15) without abandoning the 1-tap web link, and Trip Captains will still convert when their guests bear that fee rather than the Captain paying a flat booking fee.

## 5. One-page recommendation

### Pricing Recommendation: Roamly Groups

#### Executive Summary & Packaging Architecture

#### Stakeholder Alignment

##### Finance: Margin Expansion, Zero Balance-Sheet Risk, and Credit Payback

* **What you need to hear:** Every completed group booking expands net take-rate from ~15% to ~20% while keeping inventory liability off Roamly's balance sheet.
* **Unit economics & risk:** By charging hosts 15%–18% plus a 5% guest service fee (capped at $15 on $150+ milestone tickets), Roamly captures incremental high-margin revenue on larger average order values (e.g., $2,400+ charters vs. single-ticket bookings). Because reservations only lock once 100% of guests pay within the 48-hour hold window, Roamly carries zero stranded-inventory exposure.


* **CAC payback guardrail:** The $25 post-checkout travel credit on the *Milestone Guarantee* tier is restricted to post-payment account activation and requires tracking 90-day rebooking rates to ensure customer acquisition payback remains self-funding.



##### Sales Leadership & Host Partnerships: Higher Fill Rates and Defensible Supply Moat

* **What you need to hear:** Vetted local operators get guaranteed, pre-funded high-ticket group buyouts without listing-fee games, giving us a supply moat Batch cannot match.


* **Partner pitch:** Unlike Batch—which relies on sponsored placements and leaves organizers scrambling with Venmo—Roamly brings operators vetted, high-spend milestone groups (94% lock rate within 48 hours in our Q3 pilot) with guaranteed upfront collection.


* **Enterprise/Offsite wedge:** The *$49 VIP Concierge & Offsite* tier gives Partnerships a packaged, corporate-invoiceable SKU to close distributed team offsite coordinators and multi-experience local bundles.



##### Product Marketing (PMM): Zero-Friction PLG Conversion Against Batch

* **What you need to hear:** Keeping the Trip Captain price at **$0** preserves our core positioning wedge ("zero personal financial risk") and protects the 38-second, no-app-required guest checkout loop.


* **Audience messaging alignment:**
* *For Trip Captains:* Lead with "$0 on your personal card" and "48-hour zero-liability hold" so Batch's "free app" claim falls flat when compared against the pain of fronting $2,000+.


* *For Invited Guests:* Frame the 5% fee transparently at checkout as a small booking/split-protection fee alongside itemized vetted itinerary details, ensuring guests still convert in two taps without downloading an app.

#### Pre-Launch Validation Method

**Recommended Method: Conjoint Analysis**

* **Why Conjoint wins over Van Westendorp and Max-Diff:** Our critical pre-launch assumption tests a **multi-variable trade-off** across two distinct actors—whether Invited Guests tolerate a 5% fee (up to $15) without abandoning the 1-tap web link, and whether Trip Captains prefer guests paying 5% vs. the Captain paying a flat fee.


* **Why the alternatives fall short:** **Van Westendorp** only measures standalone price sensitivity for a single static offering and cannot isolate fee-incidence (who pays) or the value of the 48-hour hold. **Max-Diff** ranks feature preferences (e.g., 48-hour hold vs. $25 credit) without tying them to price elasticity or conversion drop-off. **Conjoint analysis** forces respondents to make realistic checkout trade-offs across fee structures (0% vs. 3% vs. 5% guest fee vs. flat Captain fee) and hold windows (24h vs. 48h), directly quantifying expected checkout abandonment before we launch.

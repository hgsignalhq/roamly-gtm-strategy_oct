# Messaging Pillars · Roamly Groups

## 1. From/To market shift

- **From, life before:** Organizing a high-stakes group trip means one person acts as the unpaid travel agent and personal bank, fronting thousands of dollars on their credit card, begging friends to pay them back on Venmo, and absorbing the financial loss when someone inevitably flakes.
- **To, life after:** Group trips lock in automatically only when every guest pays their own share upfront, turning milestone planning from a high-risk debt collection chore into a guaranteed, stress-free celebration.

## 2. Audiences

- **Number of audiences that need their own message:** 2
- **Why you picked this number:** The group booking flow has two distinct roles with completely different friction points: the Trip Captain (who carries the organizational burden and financial liability of booking) and the Invited Guest (who isn't planning the event, just deciding whether to trust an unfamiliar checkout link and commit their money).

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Audience 1: The Stressed Trip Captain coordinating high-stakes milestone celebrations (bachelor/bachelorettes, milestone birthdays, group getaways) where spend exceeds $150 per person.
- **Value prop:** Lock in unforgettable milestone group experiences with zero personal financial risk and zero awkward Venmo chasing.
- **Capabilities:** Split-at-checkout group booking where reservations only lock once every guest pays their individual share, paired with vetted, high-trust milestone experiences (private chefs, catamarans, guided tours) across 40+ top destinations.
- **Evidence:** In our Q3 Austin and Scottsdale pilot (420 group bookings), 94% of trips locked within 48 hours of link creation, with $0 in unpaid organizer balances. As beta user Maya R. (12-person bachelorette organizer) put it: 'We locked a $2,400 private catamaran in 19 hours without me putting a single dollar on my Amex or sending one Venmo reminder.'

### Audience 2: The Invited Group Guest receiving a payment link to join a milestone trip they didn't organize.
- **Value prop:** Claim your spot on a curated group trip in seconds, paying only for your own share with total transparency on what you are getting.
- **Capabilities:** One-click individual checkout links with clear, itemized itineraries of vetted local experiences, removing the need to download a social planning app or create an account just to pay.
- **Evidence:** Across 3,100+ guest checkouts, average payment completion time is 38 seconds with a 4.9/5 guest trust rating on vetted experiences. Beta guest Jordan T. noted: 'I paid my exact $185 share in two taps, saw the full itinerary upfront, and didn't get hit with a random Splitwise request three weeks later.
